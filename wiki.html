Welcome to the RoBlocks Wiki

RoBlocks is an independent, browser-based user-generated content (UGC) gaming platform engineered by Zakir Studio. It enables users on PC, iPad, and mobile devices to launch cross-platform 3D worlds instantly through a single web URL.

💡 Developer Notice: RoBlocks is completely independent. It is designed around universal access, completely removing the hassle of third-party App Store approval gates or local setup processes.

Zakir (Developer Profile)

Zakir is the Founder, CEO, and Principal Systems Architect behind Zakir Studio (u/ZakirStudioOfficial). He officially began development on the RoBlocks engine architecture on October 19, 2024.

Platform Contributions

Zakir handles the front-end layout trees, responsive multi-device canvas optimization, simulated Ro-Bucks (R$) transaction logic, and responsive touch pad overlays for mobile tablet clients.
